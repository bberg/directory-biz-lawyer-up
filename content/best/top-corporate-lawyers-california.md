## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a corporate lawyer do?
A corporate lawyer advises businesses on entity formation, corporate governance, securities and financing, mergers and acquisitions, contracts, and regulatory compliance. Many also handle or coordinate business litigation.

### How do I find the best corporate lawyer in California?
Start by defining your need (formation, M&A, contracts, disputes), confirm the firm serves your region, and verify relevant experience. Comparing several firms and their fee structures helps you find the best fit.

### Do corporate lawyers handle litigation?
Some do, and some focus on transactional work and bring in litigators when needed. Firms like Watkins Firm and California Business Lawyer & Corporate Lawyer combine corporate counsel with litigation defense.
