## How to Compare Norwood Legal Alternatives

1. **Business Focus** - Prefer firms with a clear business and corporate practice for company matters.
2. **Service Match** - Contracts, formation, employment, and transactions each need different strengths.
3. **Transactional vs. Litigation** - Decide whether you primarily need deal work or dispute resolution.
4. **Region** - Confirm the firm serves your state and understands local requirements.
5. **Fees** - Compare flat-fee, hourly, and retainer arrangements.
