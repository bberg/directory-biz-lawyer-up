## What Startups Should Look for in a Lawyer

1. **Entity and Equity** - Help choosing a structure and setting up a clean cap table and founder agreements.
2. **Fundraising Experience** - Familiarity with venture capital, SAFEs, convertible notes, and securities law.
3. **IP Protection** - Trademarks, IP assignment, and confidentiality from day one.
4. **Practical Contracts** - Fast, clear customer, vendor, and employment agreements.
5. **Flexible Fees** - Many startups prefer flat fees or fractional general counsel over open-ended hourly billing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should a startup hire a lawyer?
Ideally before incorporating and issuing equity. Early legal help with formation, founder agreements, and IP assignment prevents costly cleanup during fundraising or acquisition.

### How much does a startup lawyer cost?
Many startup-focused firms offer flat fees for formation and financing packages, plus fractional or "outside general counsel" arrangements so early-stage companies can access counsel without a full-time hire.

### Do I need a startup lawyer or a general business lawyer?
Look for a firm with specific startup and fundraising experience. Equity, securities, and venture financing have nuances that general practitioners may not handle regularly.
