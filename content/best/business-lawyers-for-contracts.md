## What to Look for in a Contracts Lawyer

1. **Drafting Clarity** - Clear, enforceable language that reflects your actual deal.
2. **Risk Allocation** - Thoughtful indemnification, limitation of liability, and termination terms.
3. **Industry Familiarity** - Knowledge of standard terms and pitfalls in your sector.
4. **Review Turnaround** - Reasonable speed for time-sensitive deals.
5. **Dispute Readiness** - Contracts built to hold up if a dispute arises.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to have a lawyer draft a contract?
Many business lawyers offer flat fees for standard agreements and hourly billing for complex or heavily negotiated contracts. Costs depend on length, complexity, and how much negotiation is involved.

### Should I have a lawyer review a contract before signing?
Yes, especially for significant commercial, vendor, partnership, or employment agreements. A review can catch unfavorable terms, missing protections, and ambiguous language before you are bound.

### What contracts does a small business commonly need?
Common needs include client and vendor agreements, employment and contractor agreements, NDAs, partnership or operating agreements, and terms of service. A business lawyer can prioritize what your company needs first.
